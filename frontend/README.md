## Insights

### Departments with averages above 80

This insight allows us to easily view departments with high, or above average class averages. This is important as it allows course planners and oversight teams to easily identify departments with potential grade inflation issues and take action to address them. 


### Seats by Room type

This insight allows instructors and facility planners easily view the seat count each room has. This helps instructors select classrooms of the correct size for their classes, optimizing teaching space.


### Instructors with most failed grades

This insight allows for departments to easily find instructors with high numbers of failed students. This helps departments determine which instructors, and by extension, the courses the instructors are teaching, have high fail rates and allows them to take action to make changes, such as by changing the course syllabus or reassigning instructors to different courses in their specialization.
